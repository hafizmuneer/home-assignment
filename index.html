01 / ABOUT US

What Is Embroidery Digitizing?

Embroidery digitizing is the process of converting artwork, logos and images into digital stitch files that embroidery machines can read.

Every stitch, direction and design detail matters. We combine creativity with technology to create beautiful embroidery results.
